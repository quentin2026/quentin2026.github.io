Guanting Liu

First-year PhD student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

I work under the supervision of Prof. Shaonan Wang at PolyU and Prof. Chaoming Wang at the Guangdong Institute of Intelligence Science and Technology.

Selected work

Research

Connectome

2026 - Present

Task-driven whole-brain modelling

Building mesoscale structural scaffolds from MRI connectomes and coupling regional firing-rate dynamics to cognitive tasks. I study how distributed brain networks support efficient signal propagation and reliable information routing.

2025 - Present

Multi-level cognitive computational modelling

Developing biologically constrained models, including leaky integrate-and-fire and Hodgkin-Huxley systems, to investigate working memory and decision-making. Neural-manifold analysis connects computational principles across levels of description.

Neural dynamics
Whole-brain simulation

2025 - 2026

Whole-brain simulation in model organisms

Constructed a Drosophila whole-brain model using a 140,000-neuron connectome to predict resting-state activity. I also modelled zebrafish neural activity from cellular-resolution calcium imaging and analysed sensory-motor function.

2021 - 2025

Neural decoding for brain-machine interfaces

Designed and analysed decoding algorithms for invasive motor BCI and non-invasive EEG. This work includes interpretable Kalman-filter approaches, spiking neural networks, and graph-based deep learning for motor imagery and epilepsy detection.
